The challenge

Complex maintenance deserves a simpler workflow.

Aircraft technicians operate in demanding environments where every action matters. Critical instructions, inspections and documentation are often spread across different systems and devices.

AeroVision is being designed to bring the right information directly into the technician's field of view, when and where it is needed.

01
Information at the point of work

Reduce interruptions caused by switching between tools, manuals and the aircraft.

02
Confidence in every step

Support procedure execution with contextual visual guidance and checkpoints.

03
Evidence built into the process

Make maintenance documentation part of the workflow rather than an afterthought.

Our solution

One connected experience. From instruction to evidence.

AeroVision is a proposed software platform for hands-free, aviation-specific maintenance workflows. Industrial smart glasses are the interface; the real value is the intelligence behind them.

⌗

AR-guided procedures

Contextual instructions and visual overlays help technicians navigate complex maintenance tasks without constantly looking away.

◎

Computer vision assistance

Visual recognition and proposed verification checkpoints help support consistent execution and quality processes.

▤

Digital traceability

Capture structured evidence as tasks progress, with a long-term vision of integration into existing maintenance systems.

How it works

Built around the technician. Not the device.

Our product concept follows the natural flow of a maintenance procedure, using familiar industrial wearables as an entry point.

STEP 01

Wear

Use compatible industrial smart glasses to access information hands-free.

STEP 02

Guide

Follow visual instructions relevant to the aircraft and task.

STEP 03

Verify

Use visual checkpoints to support task completion and review.

STEP 04

Record

Build a structured digital record of the work performed.

About AeroVision

Aviation expertise meets digital innovation.

AeroVision is an early-stage aerospace venture concept exploring how augmented reality and computer vision can support the people who maintain aircraft.

Our ambition is to develop practical, aviation-focused tools that complement technician expertise and work with existing maintenance processes.

Technology should work with the technician, not ask the technician to work around it.
